Your commitment

This tracker holds you to the plan you made. Each day is a new opportunity to show up — not perfectly, but honestly. The score isn't about being 100%. It's about not looking away.

What you promised her
  • Full transparency on all devices — she has access whenever she wants
  • No pornography, webcam sites, escort contacts, or the Arizona contact
  • Accountability software active and reporting
  • Weekly individual therapy, focused on ADHD and dopamine-seeking patterns
  • Couples therapy with a betrayal trauma specialist
  • Answer all questions without defensiveness, no matter when they come
  • Let her lead on intimacy — no pressure, ever
  • Full financial transparency at all times
  • If this doesn't work, a postnuptial agreement protecting her fully
When it gets hard

You will have days where the urge to seek external stimulation is strong. ADHD dopamine-seeking behavior doesn't disappear — it gets managed. On those days, the plan is:

  • Name the trigger out loud or write it down before acting on anything
  • Call your therapist or sponsor before making a choice you'll regret
  • Do not isolate — tell her you're struggling if you can
  • Log the day anyway, even if it wasn't perfect
Data & privacy

All data is saved locally on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can export your log as a text file to share with your therapist.
